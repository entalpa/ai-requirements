# The Prompt Library: AI-Driven Development (ADD)

Welcome to the heart of the ADD methodology. This folder contains "Lite" versions of the proprietary frameworks that power the **entalpa** engine. 

While most "AI prompts" focus on creative writing, these are engineered for **precision, traceability, and architectural integrity.**

---

## Quick Start: The 3-Step Workflow

To get the most out of these prompts, follow this sequence to move from a raw idea to a production-ready specification.

### **Step 1: The Foundation**
Use the **[Vibe-to-Spec Transformer](./vibe-to-spec-lite.md)**.
* **Goal:** Turn your messy "brain dump" or project description into a structured list of Stakeholders and User Stories.
* **Result:** You now have a clear map of *who* the system is for and *what* they need to do.

### **Step 2: The Elicitation**
Use the **[Socratic Architect](./socratic-architect-lite.md)**.
* **Goal:** Challenge your foundation. This prompt acts as a Senior Consultant, finding the "unknown unknowns" and logical gaps in your Step 1 output.
* **Result:** You’ll uncover missing features (security, edge cases, admin tools) that you would have otherwise forgotten.

### **Step 3: The Specification**
Use the **[Story-to-Spec Transformer](./story-to-spec-transformer-lite.md)**.
* **Goal:** Convert your refined User Stories into formal technical requirements ("The system shall...").
* **Result:** A set of atomic, testable requirements ready to be fed into an AI coder (like Cursor) or a development team.

---

## Why Use These Frameworks?

Most developers fail with AI because they provide too little context. These prompts enforce the **Maieutic Method**—a Socratic dialogue where the AI doesn't just "guess" what you want, but helps you "give birth" to a complete, logical system design.

---

## The "Manual Friction" Warning

These manual prompts are excellent for learning the methodology, but they eventually hit a "Manual Scaling Ceiling":

* **Context Drift:** In a chat window, the AI will eventually lose track of earlier constraints.
* **No Persistence:** You cannot easily "Lock" a requirement to prevent the AI from changing it in a later turn.
* **No Memory:** The AI won't remember which stories you rejected in previous sessions.
